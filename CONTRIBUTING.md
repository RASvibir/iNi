# Contributing to iNi

iNi is community by **practice**, not membership theater.

## Ways to contribute

1. **Archive protocol (preferred for opt-in)** — on [0?0](https://rasvibir.github.io/uXu/), use Quick Nav → iNi Provenance (or type `INI`) and fill `uxu.ini` fields in your archive.
2. **Community paper trail** — open a PR that adds a Markdown note under `content/articles/`.
3. **Site / docs** — improve the public site or charter; keep voice honest and non-corporate.

## Article PRs

1. Fork this repo
2. Copy `content/articles/_TEMPLATE.md` → `content/articles/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`
3. Fill front matter and sections honestly
4. Open a pull request describing what you are attesting

Do **not** claim legal chain-of-custody. Do **not** treat a merge as enrollment.

## Voice

- Opt-in and honesty first
- No studio / company administration framing
- Do not blur **uXu**, **0?0**, and **iNi**
- Keep `/qtp_[devo]` as a quiet steward signal if mentioned — not the center
