# iNi beside uXu — community charter attestation

Seed note for the provenance community. This documents how iNi sits beside the
uXu archive commons — practice first, no membership theater.

## Origin

iNi emerged as the opt-in provenance ethic beside uXu: an “I and I” framing
where trusted collaborators treat origin, authorship, lineage, and custody as
first-class responsibilities. It is not a studio brand and not required to
publish on uXu.

## Authorship

Community charter text lives in this repo (`charter/INI-PROVENANCE.md`) and is
mirrored for operators in the uXu RTFM. Public notes for visitors:
https://soloist.ai/uxu

## Custody

Stewardship of this community paper trail: maintainers of
[RASvibir/iNi](https://github.com/RASvibir/iNi). Archive opt-in remains with
each archive’s steward via `data.json` / 0?0.
